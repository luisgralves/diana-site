What is a Future Self?

A short story Diana writes from your manifestation, in which it has already happened, read to you in a calm voice. Close your eyes and listen; it takes about two minutes.

How do I create one?

In the Manifest tab, tap +, write what you want, choose a life area and tap “Create my Future Self”. The story arrives in seconds; Diana’s voice follows within a minute.

Why can’t I create another one this week?

Diana writes up to four Future Selves per week, so each one gets the care it deserves. A new one becomes available as the week rolls on.

Can I listen with the screen locked?

Yes. Once Diana’s voice is ready it keeps playing in the background and shows on your lock screen.

How do I keep a topic out of my stories?

Profile › Never Include. Diana will never mention anything on that list.

How does the Daily Journal work?

Four short steps — Reflect, Intend, Choose, Commit. In the morning you promise one small thing; in the evening you look back on your day. Each day you show up lights a flame.

How do I get a copy of my data, or delete it?

Settings › Download my data gives you everything Diana keeps. Settings › Delete Account erases your account from our servers and your device.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In your App Store or Google Play account settings, under Subscriptions.
